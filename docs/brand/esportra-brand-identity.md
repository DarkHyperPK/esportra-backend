# Esportra Brand Identity Guide

**For:** Social Media Graphics & External Creative Assets  
**Last Updated:** July 2026

---

## Brand Overview

Esportra is a competitive esports tournament platform. The brand should feel **premium, technical, and confident** — not playful or casual. Think professional motorsport energy, not gaming YouTuber.

**Brand personality:**
- Competitive, not aggressive
- Premium, not exclusive
- Technical, not complicated
- Confident, not arrogant

**Tagline options (unofficial, for creative context):**
- "Your competitive journey starts here"
- "The premier esports tournament platform"

### Color Rules

1. **Rose is the only accent.** Do not introduce greens, blues, purples, or ambers.
2. **No rainbow palettes.** Esportra is not a multi-color brand.
3. **Dark mode first.** Most assets should be matte black background with white/rose elements.
4. **Gradients are allowed** but only using rose variants (light to dark rose, or rose to transparent). No surface gradients — only for overlays, highlights, and accents.

## Typography

### Font Stack

| Role | Font | Weight | Style |
|------|------|--------|-------|
| **Headlines** | Poppins | 700–800 (Bold/ExtraBold) | Uppercase, tight tracking |
| **Body Text** | Inter | 400–600 (Regular/SemiBold) | Sentence case |
| **Labels/Badges** | Monospace (system or Consolas) | 700 (Bold) | Uppercase, wide tracking |

### Font Sources
- Poppins: [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Poppins)
- Inter: [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Inter)

### Typography Scale (reference)

| Element | Size | Weight | Notes |
|---------|------|--------|-------|
| Hero headline | 48–72px | ExtraBold | Uppercase, -0.5px letter-spacing |
| Section headline | 32–48px | Bold | Uppercase |
| Subheadline | 18–24px | SemiBold | Sentence case |
| Body | 14–16px | Regular | Left-aligned |
| Caption/label | 10–12px | Bold | Uppercase, wide tracking |

### Typography Rules

1. **Headlines are always uppercase** — no exceptions for social graphics.
2. **Body text is sentence case** — never all-caps for paragraphs.
3. **Left-align long text** — never center-align more than 2 lines.
4. **No decorative fonts** — Poppins and Inter only.

## Visual Style

### Design Philosophy

The Esportra visual style is **sharp, minimal, and high-contrast**. It draws from technical/motorsport aesthetics rather than colorful gaming culture.

**Core principles:**
- Sharp corners (no rounded edges except the app's navbar pill)
- High contrast (white on black, rose as pop)
- Minimal decoration (no unnecessary elements)
- Bold typography (headlines command attention)

### Do's

- Use sharp, geometric compositions
- Embrace negative space
- Let the rose accent draw the eye
- Use high-quality esports photography (if using imagery)
- Apply subtle grain/noise textures for depth (optional)
- Use the rose-to-transparent gradient as an overlay on photos

### Don'ts

- No rounded corners on graphics or frames
- No glow effects or drop shadows on text
- No gradients as surface fills (only as overlays)
- No additional accent colors
- No busy patterns or backgrounds
- No clip art or low-quality game renders
- No "gamer" cliches (neon, RGB rainbows, flames)

### Image Treatment

When using photography:
1. Desaturate slightly (−10 to −20% saturation)
2. Apply matte black gradient overlay from edges
3. Optional: rose-tinted gradient overlay for accent
4. High contrast adjustments

**Subject matter:**
- Competitive players (focused, intense expressions)
- Tournament venues and stages
- Hardware and setups (clean, premium)
- Abstract esports imagery (minimal, geometric)

**Avoid:**
- Casual gaming imagery
- Overly saturated game screenshots
- Stock photos with fake "gamer" energy

## Social Media Applications

### Profile Pictures
- Use icon mark on matte black background
- Keep it simple — logo mark only, no text

### Post Templates

**Announcement posts:**
- Matte black background
- White headline (Poppins, uppercase)
- Rose accent line or element
- Logo in corner

**Tournament graphics:**
- Game artwork as background (desaturated, with dark overlay)
- White text for tournament name
- Rose accent for dates/CTA
- Clean, readable hierarchy

**Stats/Results:**
- Matte black background
- Large white numbers (Poppins)
- Rose highlights for key metrics
- Minimal supporting text

### Stories/Reels

- Maintain brand colors even in video
- Text overlays: white Poppins on semi-transparent black
- Transitions: clean cuts or simple fades (no flashy effects)
- Lower thirds: rose accent bar with white text

## Voice & Tone

### Writing Style

**Tone:** Professional, direct, confident. Not corporate stiff, but not casual either.

**Voice characteristics:**
- Informative over hype
- Respectful to competitors
- Inclusive of all skill levels
- Technical when needed, plain when possible

### Examples

**Good:**
- "Registration opens Friday at 6 PM UTC."
- "256 teams. One champion. The bracket is live."
- "GG to [Team] — see you in the finals."

**Avoid:**
- "OMG YOU WON'T BELIEVE THIS INSANE TOURNAMENT!!!"
- "Let's gooooo gamers!"
- "Drop a comment if you're hyped!"

### Hashtags

Primary: `#Esportra`  
Secondary: `#EsportraTournaments` `#CompeteOnEsportra`

Game-specific: Combine with game tags (e.g., `#Esportra #VALORANT`)
